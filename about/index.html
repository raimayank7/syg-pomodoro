Pomodoro

About Pomodoro

Pomodoro is a free focus timer with guides for studying and working in short timed rounds. It has the classic rhythm of 25 minutes of work and 5 minutes of rest, longer rhythms for deep work, a planner that turns a list of tasks into a timetable, and printable sheets.

How it is written

Every guide is our own writing, based on how the method works and on plain practical experience of studying and working with a timer. We make no medical or scientific claims and quote no studies. Where a page suggests round lengths, for example for children, it says that these are starting points to adjust, not rules.

The name

The method was created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. "Pomodoro" is Italian for tomato, after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used. Your data

The timer settings, the task list, the daily counts and the planner entries are kept in your own browser storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing the site data in your browser removes them.
